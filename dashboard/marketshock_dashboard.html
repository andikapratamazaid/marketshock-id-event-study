MarketShock ID: Indonesia Market Reaction to the Vice Presidential Resignation

An event study with a global investor lens. Daily market data downloaded on 17 November 2026 (Yahoo Finance). Research question: did global investors read the 12 November 2026 resignation of Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka as risk relief or as added uncertainty?

Attribution warning. MSCI announced its index review results on the same day as the resignation, 12 November 2026 (BRPT and BRMS removed from the Global Standard Index, effective 1 December 2026). Reading: global investors (EIDO) matched the local relief move on the day (+2.55% vs +2.66%) and lagged only modestly over the following days, part of which is currency translation in a dollar-denominated fund. Over the 11 to 16 November bracket the rupiah weakened slightly less than the broad dollar move, so the downloaded data show no added Indonesia-specific currency premium across that span. Reported spot prints on 12 November itself (about 18,140 to 18,250) and the reported rise in the SBN 10-year yield to 7.63% point to more caution in currency and rates than in equities.

Rolling volatility

IHSG volatility was already extreme before the event (53.3% annualised on 11 November) because of the late-October selloff, and peaked at 54.5% on 12 November. USD/IDR volatility readings that would span the invalid closes are not computable and are left blank by construction.

Conclusion, bounded by the data

No panic, and a measurable relief reaction in equities shared by local and global investors: IHSG +2.66% and EIDO +2.55% on 12 November with reported foreign net buying of Rp443.53 billion. Currency and rates evidence, which rests partly on reporting, shows more caution. The structural backdrop remains a market down -39.27% year-to-date and about Rp127 trillion of reported foreign net selling in 2026, which one calm week does not reverse. Because of the same-day MSCI announcement, none of the rally can be credited to the resignation alone. Full method, limitations, and sources: see the project README and findings report.
