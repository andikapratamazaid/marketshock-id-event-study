# Event Timeline: The November 2026 Vice Presidential Resignation

All dates and facts below are taken from Indonesian news reporting and official
fact-check channels. The tone is strictly factual. This document is the readable
version of `data/events_timeline.csv`.

## Background: the hoax waves came first

Claims that Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka had resigned circulated twice
before anything actually happened:

- **May 2026.** A TikTok video dated 11 May 2026 claimed Gibran had sent a
  resignation letter to the DPR RI. Provincial fact-check channels (Jabar Saber
  Hoaks, Jatengprov) clarified that the footage was from **16 July 2024**, when
  Gibran resigned as Mayor of Solo ahead of his inauguration as Vice President.
  The claim was a hoax with recycled video.
- **Late September to October 2026.** The claim resurfaced alongside the
  Constitutional Court proceedings on the challenge to Gibran's education
  requirements in the 2024 election. A chain message claimed he resigned on
  9 October 2026. Kompas.com's fact-check desk published a debunking of the
  related video claim on **30 September 2026**. Projo chairman Budi Arie Setiadi
  publicly called the resignation messages a hoax.

These waves matter for an event study because they show the market and the
public had been exposed to the resignation narrative, in false form, months
before the real event.

## The legal and political sequence

- **30 October 2026.** The Supreme Court (Mahkamah Agung), in cassation decision
  No. 742 K/TUN/2026, annulled the education equivalency letter (surat keterangan
  penyetaraan pendidikan) that had been used in Gibran's 2024 candidacy.
- **11 November 2026.** A total of 120 DPR members from eight factions proposed
  Hak Menyatakan Pendapat, the parliamentary opinion process that can lead to
  impeachment proceedings. On the same day, the Ministry of Primary and
  Secondary Education revoked the equivalency letter, implementing the court
  decision. Reporting also indicates Gibran had conveyed his intention to resign
  to President Prabowo Subianto on the morning of 10 November 2026.
- **12 November 2026 (main event).** Gibran arrived at the parliamentary complex
  in Senayan, Jakarta, met the MPR leadership for about 20 minutes, and handed
  over his resignation letter. MPR Chair Ahmad Muzani confirmed publicly that
  the MPR had received the letter. Gibran also delivered the letter to President
  Prabowo Subianto at the Merdeka Palace and stated, in a video on the Vice
  Presidential Secretariat channel, that the decision was personal and, in his
  words, not related to the education document issue. He had been in office
  since 20 October 2024, roughly two years of a term scheduled to run to 2029.
- **13 November 2026.** The MPR leadership held a joint meeting to prepare the
  process of filling the vacancy. Under Article 8 paragraph (2) of the 1945
  Constitution, the MPR must convene within 60 days to elect a new Vice
  President from two candidates proposed by the President.

## What the market did on 12 November 2026 (reported figures)

Reported by national financial outlets (see `data/reported_figures.csv` for the
sourced values):

- The Jakarta Composite Index (IHSG) **rose 2.66 percent to close at 5,250.90**,
  led by energy and financial stocks.
- The rupiah **weakened** to roughly Rp18,216 per US dollar (prints between
  about Rp18,140 and Rp18,250 across outlets), close to its record weakest
  level of about Rp18,279.
- The 10-year government bond (SBN) yield **rose to 7.63 percent**, up about
  7.9 basis points.
- Foreign investors recorded **net buying of Rp443.53 billion** in equities that
  day, against cumulative net selling of roughly **Rp127 trillion** since the
  start of 2026.

## The confounder that limits every conclusion

On the same day, 12 November 2026, MSCI announced the results of its index
review: BRPT and BRMS were removed from the MSCI Global Standard Index (BRMS
moved to Small Cap) and seven stocks left the Small Cap index, effective
1 December 2026. Technical rebound dynamics after a year-to-date IHSG decline
of more than 39 percent were also cited by analysts. Because these factors
coincide with the resignation, **no part of the 12 November market move can be
attributed to the resignation alone**. This study therefore describes market
behaviour around the events and separates global from domestic price pressure;
it does not claim single-cause attribution.
